Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NDA photo size for online application?
UPSC NDA online application typically requires a passport-size colour photo of 400×400 pixels, 20–100 KB in JPG with a plain white background.
What is the NDA signature size?
NDA signature: approximately 400×200 pixels, 10–50 KB in JPG. Sign clearly on white paper in black ink.
Can girls apply for NDA and submit a photo?
Yes. Female candidates have been eligible for NDA since 2022. The same photo requirements apply.
What kind of photo should I use for NDA?
A formal, front-facing colour photo in a collared shirt or formal attire. Plain white background. No cap, sunglasses or jewellery. Direct gaze at the camera.
Does NDA require a recent photo?
Yes. Use a recent passport-size photo taken within the last 6 months. The same photo must be used throughout the NDA selection process (SSB, medical, etc.).
What is the NDA age limit?
NDA has a strict age window: 16.5 to 19.5 years on the first day of the month of course commencement. There is no age relaxation for any category. Check our NDA age calculator.
