Why Convert Images to PDF?

Many Indian university admission portals, scholarship applications and government recruitment forms ask for supporting documents (Aadhaar, mark sheets, certificates, photos) uploaded as a single PDF file. If you have these as separate JPG/PNG images, converting them to PDF is the fastest way to comply.

PDF is also a more reliable document format than image files — it preserves layout, doesn't lose quality when shared, and is accepted by virtually all portals and email clients.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do portals ask for documents as PDF?
PDF is a universal document format accepted by all portals. It preserves quality better than images when printed or digitally shared. Government and university portals prefer it for document uploads (Aadhaar, certificates, marksheets).

Each image is fitted to an A4 page (210×297 mm) while maintaining its original aspect ratio.
