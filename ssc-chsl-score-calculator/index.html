How the SSC CHSL score is calculated

Example: 15 correct and 5 wrong in a section → (15 × 2) − (5 × 0.5) = 27.5 marks. Each wrong answer costs 0.5 mark(s) — 25% of what a correct answer earns — so guess only when you can eliminate options.
