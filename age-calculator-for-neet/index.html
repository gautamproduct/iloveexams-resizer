About NEET Undergraduate Medical Entrance Examination Age Eligibility

The age limit for NEET Undergraduate Medical Entrance Examination is strictly enforced. Unlike marks or qualifications, there is no provision for relaxation beyond the stated limits (except for reserved categories as tabulated above). Missing the eligibility window by even one day means you cannot apply.

Your age is counted as of 31 December of the admission year. This means it is the age you are on that date that matters — not the date you fill the form or the date of the exam.

Important reminder

Age limits change with each official notification. Use this calculator to get an estimate, but always verify your eligibility from the official NEET-UG notification published on the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NEET age limit 2026-2027?
The minimum age for NEET-UG is 17 years as on 31 December of the year of admission. There is NO upper age limit for NEET-UG after the Supreme Court struck down the 25-year upper age limit in 2018.
Is there an upper age limit for NEET?
No upper age limit exists for NEET-UG after the Supreme Court of India's ruling in 2018. Any candidate who is at least 17 years old on 31 December of the admission year can appear.
How many times can I attempt NEET?
There is no attempt limit for NEET-UG. You can appear in NEET as many times as you wish, as long as you are at least 17 years old.
What is the minimum age to appear in NEET?
You must be at least 17 years old on 31 December of the year in which you are seeking admission (not the year of the exam). If your birthday is after 31 December, you will not qualify for that admission cycle.
What subjects are compulsory for NEET eligibility?
Physics, Chemistry and Biology/Biotechnology are compulsory in Class 12 (10+2). Minimum PCB aggregate: 50% for general, 40% for SC/ST/OBC, 45% for PwBD general category.
What is the NEET photo size requirement?
NEET online application form requires a recent colour photo, 10×12 cm equivalent, 10–200 KB in JPG format with white background. Signature: 3.5×1.5 cm, under 30 KB. Check the NTA notification each year.
Can I apply for NEET if I am still in Class 11?
No. You must be in Class 12 or have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology as mandatory subjects.
