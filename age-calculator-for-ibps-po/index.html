About IBPS Probationary Officer (PO) Examination Age Eligibility

The age limit for IBPS Probationary Officer (PO) Examination is strictly enforced. Unlike marks or qualifications, there is no provision for relaxation beyond the stated limits (except for reserved categories as tabulated above). Missing the eligibility window by even one day means you cannot apply.

Your age is counted as of notification date (varies annually). This means it is the age you are on that date that matters — not the date you fill the form or the date of the exam.

Important reminder

Age limits change with each official notification. Use this calculator to get an estimate, but always verify your eligibility from the official IBPS PO notification published on the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBPS PO age limit 2025?
IBPS PO age limit is 20–30 years as of the notification date for general category candidates. OBC gets +3 years (up to 33), SC/ST gets +5 years (up to 35).
What is the age cut-off date for IBPS PO?
Age for IBPS PO eligibility is calculated as of the date specified in the notification — usually the opening date of the application form. This date changes each year.
Is there an attempt limit for IBPS PO?
No. There is no limit on the number of times you can attempt IBPS PO, as long as you meet the age and qualification criteria each time you apply.
What qualification is needed for IBPS PO?
A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university (or its equivalent as recognised by the Central Government).
What is the IBPS PO photo size requirement?
IBPS PO requires a photo of approximately 200×230 pixels, 20–50 KB in JPG. Signature: approximately 80×30 pixels, under 20 KB. Check the current year notification for exact specs.
Can I apply for IBPS PO in my final year of graduation?
Yes. Final-year students can apply, but they must have completed the degree before the date of interview/joining specified in the notification.
What is the difference between IBPS PO and IBPS Clerk age limit?
IBPS PO: 20–30 years (general). IBPS Clerk: 20–28 years (general). Both have the same OBC/SC/ST relaxations, but the maximum age for Clerk is 2 years lower.
