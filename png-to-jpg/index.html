Why Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG is a lossless format — it preserves every pixel perfectly and supports transparency. However, PNG files are significantly larger than JPEG for photographs. A typical exam photo saved as PNG can be 500 KB–2 MB, while the same photo as JPEG at 85% quality is typically under 100 KB.

Indian exam portals (UPSC, SSC, IBPS, NTA, RRB) almost exclusively accept JPEG/JPG format. If your photo is a PNG, you'll need to convert it before you can upload it to most application forms.

What about transparency?

JPEG does not support transparent pixels. If your PNG has a transparent background (common for logos, screenshots and edited photos), the converter fills the transparent areas with your chosen background colour. For exam photos, white is always the correct choice.
