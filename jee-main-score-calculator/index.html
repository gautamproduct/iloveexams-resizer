How the JEE Main score is calculated

Example: 15 correct and 5 wrong in a section → (15 × 4) − (5 × 1) = 55 marks. Each wrong answer costs 1 mark(s) — 25% of what a correct answer earns — so guess only when you can eliminate options.

FAQs

What is the JEE Main marking scheme?
+4 for each correct answer and −1 for each wrong answer. Unattempted questions get 0.
How many questions are in JEE Main?
JEE Main Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech): 75 questions, 300 marks. Duration: 3 hours.
Is this JEE Main score the final score?
It is your raw score from the answer key. The official result may apply normalisation, bonus marks for dropped questions, or conversion to percentiles.
Is the calculator free and private?
Yes — it runs in your browser and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
