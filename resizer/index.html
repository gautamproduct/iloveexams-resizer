Is my photo uploaded to any server?
No. The resizer runs entirely in your browser — your photo and signature never leave your device.
Can I remove the background from my exam photo?
Yes. After uploading, tap Remove BG to make the background plain white using on-device AI. The first use downloads the model, which takes a few seconds.

Yes. Every preset can be edited — change width, height, minimum and maximum KB, or switch to cm, mm or inches, if your notification differs.
How do I reduce my photo below 50 KB?

Does ILoveExams work on mobile phones?
Yes — it works in Chrome and Safari on Android and iPhone, and HEIC photos from iPhones are converted automatically.
