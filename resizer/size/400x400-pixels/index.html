FAQs

How do I resize a photo to 400×400 pixels?
Upload it in the tool on this page. The crop box is locked to 400:400; tap Process and the image is saved as a 400×400 px JPG within 20–300 KB. You can change the KB range in the tool.
What is 400×400 pixels in cm?
It depends on DPI: 10.58 × 10.58 cm at 96 DPI, 5.08 × 5.08 cm at 200 DPI and 3.39 × 3.39 cm at 300 DPI. Exam portals check pixels, not cm.
Is 400×400 width × height or height × width?
Width first: 400 px wide and 400 px tall (a square image).
