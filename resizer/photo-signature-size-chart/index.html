FAQs

What is the standard photo size for government exams in India?
There is no single standard. The most common sizes are 200×230 px (20–50 KB) for bank exams (IBPS, SBI, RBI) and 275×354 px (20–50 KB) for SSC, NTA and many State PSC exams. Always use your exam's exact size from the chart.
What is the standard signature size for government exams?
Bank exams usually ask for 140×60 px, 10–20 KB. SSC CGL/CPO/JE use 236×79 px, 10–20 KB, and NTA exams (JEE, NEET) use 275×118 px.

What format should exam photos and signatures be in?
Almost every Indian exam portal accepts only JPG/JPEG. Convert PNG, HEIC or WEBP images to JPG — the resizer does this automatically.
