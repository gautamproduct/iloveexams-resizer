How Age is Calculated for Indian Competitive Exams

Most Indian competitive exams calculate your age as of a specific cut-off date stated in the official notification — not on the date of the exam or the date you fill the form. Common cut-off dates are 1 January or 1 August of the exam year.

This means: if the cut-off is 1 August 2025 and the upper limit is 32, you must have been born on or after 2 August 1993. Being even one day older makes you ineligible for that attempt.

Age relaxation for reserved categories

  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer): +3 years over the general upper limit
  • SC / ST: +5 years over the general upper limit
  • PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities): +10 years (general), +13 years (OBC), +15 years (SC/ST)
  • Ex-Servicemen: varies by exam, typically 3–5 years
  • J&K residents (domicile): +5 years for certain central exams

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my exact age online?
Enter your date of birth in the calculator above, set the "age as of" date (defaults to today), and click Calculate. You will instantly see your age in years, months and days, total days lived, and days until your next birthday.
What is the age limit for UPSC CSE (IAS)?
The UPSC Civil Services Examination requires candidates to be between 21 and 32 years old as of 1 August of the exam year. OBC candidates get +3 years (up to 35), SC/ST get +5 years (up to 37), and PwBD get an additional +10 years.
What is the age limit for SSC CGL?
SSC CGL requires candidates to be between 18 and 32 years (general category) as of 1 January of the exam year. OBC gets +3 years, SC/ST gets +5 years. Post-specific age limits may vary — always check the notification.
How is age calculated for government jobs in India?
Age for Indian government jobs is calculated as of the cut-off date specified in the official notification. This is typically 1 January or 1 August of the exam year. Your exact date of birth determines eligibility — partial years are counted in years and completed months.
What is the age limit for IBPS PO?
IBPS PO age limit is 20–30 years for general category candidates as on the notification date. OBC gets +3 years (20–33), SC/ST gets +5 years (20–35).
Does age relaxation apply automatically if I belong to OBC/SC/ST?
Age relaxation is applied only if you select and claim it during the application process and provide valid supporting documents (caste/category certificate). It is not applied automatically.
Is this age calculator accurate?
Yes. The calculator computes the exact difference between your date of birth and the selected date in years, months and days, accounting for varying month lengths and leap years.
Can I check my eligibility for multiple exams at once?
Yes. After you calculate your age, the tool shows a quick eligibility check for 10+ major Indian competitive exams based on the general category age range. Enter your DOB and click Calculate to see the results.
