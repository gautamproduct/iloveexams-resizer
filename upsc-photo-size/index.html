Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UPSC CSE photo size in pixels?
UPSC CSE typically requires a photo of 400×400 pixels (or as specified in the current notification), 20–300 KB in JPG format with a white background.
What is the UPSC signature size?
The UPSC online form requires a scanned signature image of 400×400 pixels, 20–100 KB in JPG. Sign on plain white paper in black ink and scan at 200 DPI or higher.
Can I use a mobile photo for UPSC CSE application?
Yes, if the photo meets the specifications — white background, proper lighting, full face visible, 400×400 px and under 300 KB in JPG. Use our resize tool to convert any photo.
What happens if I upload a wrong-sized photo for UPSC?
The portal may reject the upload outright or, in some cases, accept it and flag the application during scrutiny. Using the exact specified dimensions avoids any risk of rejection.
Does UPSC accept PNG photos?
UPSC online forms typically accept only JPG/JPEG. Convert PNG to JPG using our PNG to JPG tool before uploading.
How recent must the UPSC photo be?
UPSC recommends a photo taken within the last 3 months before filling the application form.
