IBPS Clerk Score Calculator 2026-2027

Enter your correct and wrong answers from the answer key to get your IBPS Clerk score instantly — section by section, with negative marking applied.

IBPS Clerk marking scheme: +1 for each correct answer and −0.25 for each wrong answer, and 0 for unattempted questions. The paper has 100 questions for 100 marks. Score = (correct × 1) − (wrong × 0.25).

How the IBPS Clerk score is calculated

Example: 15 correct and 5 wrong in a section → (15 × 1) − (5 × 0.25) = 13.75 marks. Each wrong answer costs 0.25 mark(s) — 25% of what a correct answer earns — so guess only when you can eliminate options.
