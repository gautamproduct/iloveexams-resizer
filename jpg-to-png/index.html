When Should You Convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is a lossless format — every pixel is stored exactly as it is, with no compression artifacts. This makes it ideal for screenshots, graphics with text, logos, and any image you plan to edit and re-save multiple times.

Converting a JPG to PNG does not improve the quality of a JPG — the original compression has already happened. It simply stores the same pixels in a lossless container. The PNG will be larger than the JPG but won't degrade further if you edit and re-save it.

Common use cases

  • Importing a photo into design software (Canva, Photoshop) that works better with PNG
  • Websites and apps that specifically require PNG format
  • Preserving quality across multiple edit-save cycles
  • Adding transparency after conversion in an image editor

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert JPG to PNG online for free?

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?
No. JPEG compression is irreversible — converting to PNG stores the same pixels without further loss, but cannot recover quality that was already lost during JPEG compression.
Will the PNG be larger than the original JPG?
Yes — PNG is lossless and stores more data. A 200 KB JPG typically becomes 500 KB–2 MB as PNG.
